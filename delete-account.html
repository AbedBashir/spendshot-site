Delete your Spendshot account

Developer: Abdel Rahman Bashir · App: Spendshot: Budget & Expenses

In the app (instant)

  1. Open Spendshot and go to the You tab.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. Enter your password (and your authenticator code if two-step verification is on) and tap Delete permanently.

Without the app

Email spendshot.app@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject “Delete my Spendshot account”. We'll confirm and delete it within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Everything linked to your account is permanently deleted: your profile (name, email, phone, country, currencies, photo), all transactions, categories, budgets, savings goals, bills, subscriptions, net-worth records and login history. Nothing is kept, except that server backups are overwritten within a further 30 days.

Premium subscribers: deleting your account doesn't cancel your subscription. Cancel it in Google Play → Subscriptions (or App Store → Subscriptions) to stop being charged.
