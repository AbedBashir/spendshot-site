Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Spendshot ("the app", "we") is a personal finance app for tracking income, spending, budgets and savings goals. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. Contact: spendshot.app@gmail.com.

Data you give us

DataWhy
Name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash)To create and secure your account
Country, phone number (optional), main and secondary currencyCurrency and number formatting, account recovery and security
Income, expenses, merchants, notes, budgets, goals and other financial entries you addThe core features of the app
Profile photo (optional)Shown in your profile
Screenshots you choose to import (Premium)To read the transactions in them for you

Spendshot does not connect to your bank and never asks for bank login details or card numbers.

Data collected automatically

  • Login history — device type and IP address of each sign-in, shown to you under Security so you can spot unknown logins.
  • Advertising data (free plan only) — Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and app interaction data to show and measure ads. See "Advertising" below.

Data that stays on your device

Face ID / fingerprint is handled entirely by your phone's operating system; Spendshot never receives or stores biometric data. Login tokens are kept in your device's secure keychain/keystore.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers that run parts of the app:

ProviderWhat and why
Anthropic (Claude AI)Screenshots you import, so the transactions in them can be extracted. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models.
Google AdMobAdvertising on the free plan (see below).
RevenueCat, Google Play, Apple App StoreProcessing and verifying Premium subscriptions. We never see your card details.
Our hosting providerStoring your account and financial entries on our servers.
ExchangeRate-API (open.er-api.com)Fetching daily exchange rates. Only the currency code is sent, never your data.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Premium subscribers see no ads. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice anytime under You → Ad privacy choices. On iPhone you are asked for tracking permission; if you decline, ads are not personalized. Learn how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. Optional two-step verification and biometric app lock are available in the app.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete individual entries anytime in the app. To delete your account and all associated data, email spendshot.app@gmail.com from your account's email address with the subject "Delete my Spendshot account"; we delete it within 30 days. Server backups are overwritten within a further 30 days.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export (CSV in the app) or delete your data, and object to or restrict its processing. Contact us at the address above. If you are in the EU/UK you may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Spendshot is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify you in the app.
